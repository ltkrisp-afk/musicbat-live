# MUSICBAT — INTEGRATED RIGHTS, MARKETPLACE & RADIO FRAMEWORK

**Document status:** Proposed legal text for integration, engineering validation, and legal review.

**Governing principle:** MusicBat manages the discovery, preservation, authorized delivery, and sale of music without acquiring ownership of the music or control over the artist's career.

## Article 1 — Artist Ownership and MusicBat's Role

The Artist and any other lawful rights holders retain their respective ownership interests in every submitted sound recording, musical composition, lyric, performance, and associated creative work.

MusicBat provides technology and administrative services for authorized music preservation, discovery, listening, digital commerce, rights documentation, and payment accounting.

Participation does not transfer copyright ownership to MusicBat, establish an exclusive recording relationship, appoint MusicBat as the Artist's career manager, or prevent the Artist from independently pursuing other lawful opportunities.

MusicBat may exercise only those permissions that have been granted by parties with sufficient legal authority and verified for the particular service.

## Article 2 — Limited Operational License

For each recording approved under the applicable rights schedule, the Artist grants MusicBat a nonexclusive license to receive, securely store, preserve, back up, technically process, reproduce, transmit, and deliver the recording and its authorized underlying musical works solely as necessary to provide the MusicBat services expressly selected and cleared for that recording.

The license is limited by the documented territory, duration, rights ownership, applicable restrictions, and authorized uses.

MusicBat may permit infrastructure providers and other necessary contractors to perform technical activities on its behalf, but those providers receive no independent right to commercially exploit the recording.

These operational permissions do not cancel compensation owed under MusicBat's purchase or streaming provisions.

No copyright ownership is transferred. No synchronization, artificial-intelligence training, unrelated advertising endorsement, or independent commercial exploitation is authorized by this provision.

## Article 3 — Artist Authority and Rights Verification

The Artist represents and warrants that the Artist owns or possesses legally sufficient authorization to grant the specific rights identified in the completed recording declaration.

The Artist must accurately identify applicable recording owners, composers, publishers, administrators, collecting societies, collaborators, ownership percentages, and restrictions affecting the requested uses.

MusicBat does not require an Artist to personally own every copyright where valid authorization has been obtained from other rights holders.

Membership in a performing-rights organization does not automatically disqualify an Artist. Any restrictions on direct licensing must be disclosed and evaluated.

MusicBat may suspend an affected use where authority is missing, disputed, withdrawn, or insufficiently documented.

The Artist agrees to cooperate in resolving legitimate rights claims and accepts responsibility for breaches of the Artist's contractual representations, subject to the agreement's remedies, applicable law, and properly reviewed indemnification provisions.

## Article 4 — Digital Download Marketplace

MusicBat facilitates authorized digital music transactions between participating Artists and purchasers.

For each completed eligible download sale, the base sale price shall be allocated:

- **85% to the Artist allocation**
- **15% to MusicBat**

Applicable taxes and separately disclosed payment-processing charges shall be accounted for in accordance with law and the applicable payment provider's requirements.

MusicBat shall not reduce the Artist's contractual allocation to cover its own operating expenses or independently incurred licensing obligations unless a separate, valid agreement expressly authorizes that treatment.

The Artist allocation shall be paid to the designated verified recipient identified in the executed payment instructions. That recipient remains responsible for distributing amounts owed to other beneficiaries under their applicable agreements.

MusicBat shall preserve purchase-linked entitlement records and support lawful redelivery, subject to copyright restrictions, legal removal obligations, and the purchaser's applicable terms.

## Article 5 — Radio Discovery and Membership

MusicBat provides free music discovery and an optional enhanced radio membership.

The enhanced membership is priced at **$5.00 per month**, subject to applicable taxes and clearly disclosed subscription conditions.

Membership grants access to designated platform features. It does not transfer ownership of recordings or constitute the purchase of an individual musical work.

A recording may participate in free or paid listening only where MusicBat has verified the permissions necessary for the actual streaming behavior, territory, and service.

Membership charges do not independently replace copyright permissions, applicable reporting requirements, or other legal obligations.

## Article 6 — Membership Revenue Reconciliation

For each monthly accounting period, MusicBat shall determine **Qualifying Collected Membership Revenue (QCMR)** using the qualifying settled membership receipts and the exclusions, reversals, and adjustments defined in the approved financial schedule.

Subject to applicable funding conditions, MusicBat shall establish a streaming rights-holder pool **not exceeding 25% of QCMR**.

This pool is separate from digital download revenue and is not governed by the 85% Artist / 15% MusicBat download allocation.

The amount allocated to an eligible recording shall be determined by dividing that recording's verified eligible listening units by the total verified eligible listening units for all qualifying recordings during the accounting period, then multiplying the result by the distributable pool.

Only listening events meeting the published eligibility rules and supported by reliable playback evidence may contribute to the calculation.

Listener voting and tuning may influence discovery and rotation. Such actions shall not independently increase the streaming pool or change monetary allocation unless a separately approved financial schedule expressly authorizes a defined weighting method.

MusicBat shall retain an auditable reconciliation record identifying qualifying revenue, adjustments, pool funding, eligible listening units, allocations, rounding adjustments, payment recipients, holds, and completed distributions.

The approved financial schedule must establish treatment of periods with no eligible listening units, refunds after settlement, disputed allocations, and undistributed balances before this provision becomes effective.

## Article 7 — Preservation and Artist Independence

MusicBat may retain recording-identification data, documented licensing permissions, transaction records, and other lawful provenance information needed to maintain accurate historical, contractual, and financial records.

Preservation of records does not confer ownership of the music.

Where an Artist withdraws a recording, MusicBat shall discontinue newly unauthorized uses according to the governing agreement while preserving records and obligations that lawfully survive withdrawal.

Historical participation does not authorize MusicBat to commercially exploit an Artist's name, likeness, personal story, or subsequent achievements without the appropriate separate permission.

## Article 8 — Copyright Complaints and Protection

MusicBat shall maintain procedures for receiving, evaluating, and responding to copyright complaints and disputes.

Where applicable, those procedures shall include a designated copyright agent, notice-and-takedown and counter-notice handling, a repeat-infringer policy, appropriate access restrictions, and preservation of relevant evidence.

MusicBat shall not treat an Artist's warranty as a substitute for its own legal responsibilities.

## Article 9 — Excluded Services

MusicBat does not offer synchronization licensing under this agreement.

No Artist submission, membership payment, digital purchase, or platform participation authorizes MusicBat to license a recording or composition for film, television, advertising, games, or other audiovisual synchronization.

Any future addition of such a service requires separate owner authorization, appropriate legal documents, and specific rights-holder consent.

## Article 10 — Activation and Contract Integrity

The operative agreement shall be the specific approved version affirmatively accepted by the authorized contracting party.

MusicBat shall record the accepted agreement version, integrity digest, signer identity and capacity, acceptance timestamp, applicable recording declarations, and relevant rights evidence.

Publication, browsing, or submission of a recording does not independently establish the permissions necessary for an uncleared use.

A new agreement version shall not silently replace an earlier executed agreement or expand permissions without legally sufficient notice and consent.

**End of proposed integrated provisions.**

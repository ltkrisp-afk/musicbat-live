## Addendum M — Marketplace and Digital Delivery
M1. MusicBat provides hosting, discovery, payment facilitation and delivery services for artist-authorized recordings. MusicBat does not purchase title to recordings or compositions merely by listing them. Artist and applicable third-party owners retain all ownership interests.
M2. For each recording and underlying composition for which the Artist demonstrates sufficient licensing authority, Artist grants MusicBat a nonexclusive, limited license, for the territories, term and uses affirmatively recorded in the executed recording schedule, to receive, store, back up, cache, reproduce, technically convert, display and deliver the work as necessary to perform each specifically authorized MusicBat service. Necessary contractors may exercise those permissions solely on MusicBat's behalf; no independent exploitation or general onward licensing is permitted. No use is authorized beyond the rights actually cleared.
M3. The operational permissions in M2 do not extinguish compensation owed under the digital-sale and radio-membership provisions. No synchronization, model training, advertising endorsement, derivative exploitation or general promotional reuse is implied.
M4. For an authorized permanent digital download, MusicBat allocates 85% of the base sale price to Artist and 15% to MusicBat. Taxes and legally disclosed buyer processing charges are accounted for separately. A verified designated recipient receives the Artist allocation under signed beneficiary instructions; MusicBat does not guarantee automated splits among collaborators. Disputes and reversals are tracked without silently reallocating earned funds.
M5. Withdrawal terminates new uses as required by the executed terms, without destroying lawful records, earned liabilities or previously granted customer entitlements. Customer refunds, delivery failures and mandatory rights are governed by applicable law and disclosed customer terms.

## Addendum R — Optional Radio Membership and Pool
R1. A listener may subscribe to MusicBat's optional $5 monthly enhanced radio membership. Renewal, cancellation, taxes and feature availability must be disclosed in the listener-facing subscription terms before enrollment. Membership purchases platform access; it does not convey ownership or a personal license to any particular song.
R2. Free discovery and paid listening are independently permissioned uses. A recording enters either channel only after the actual transmission and interaction model is cleared for the necessary recording and composition rights, territories and term. Subscription labeling does not waive external licensing obligations.
R3. For each UTC accounting month, MusicBat calculates Qualifying Collected Membership Revenue (QCMR) from actual qualifying settled membership receipts using the existing agreement's exclusions and reconciliation treatment. Subject to the agreement's funding and eligibility conditions, the total rights-holder pool shall not exceed 25% of QCMR. Neither listening volume nor voting increases this ceiling.
R4. Eligible recording allocation = distributable pool multiplied by that recording's verified eligible listening units divided by total verified eligible units in the period. Only server-correlated eligible listening evidence and fully cleared recording uses count. Free listening, self-play, overlap, missing delivery evidence and invalid repeat behavior do not count. Rounding, zero-unit months, disputes, holds, carry-forward and correction procedures must be specified consistently with Section 8 and Schedule D before activation; no undisclosed forfeiture is authorized.
R5. Voting and tuning affect discovery and rotation, not the monetary allocation formula, unless a separately reviewed and affirmatively approved schedule expressly defines eligible vote weighting. Membership receipts and streaming allocations remain separate from the 85/15 digital-sale ledger.

## Addendum W — Rights Authority, Disclosures, and Remedies
W1. Artist represents that all submitted information is accurate to Artist's knowledge after reasonable investigation, and warrants that Artist owns or has secured legally sufficient authority to grant every specific right claimed in the completed schedules. This is not a representation that the Artist personally owns 100% of every right where properly authorized co-owners or publishers exist.
W2. Artist identifies relevant recording owners, songwriters, publishers, administrators, PRO/collecting-society affiliations, sample/cover/beat licenses, restrictions, territories, collaborator allocations and designated collection authority. PRO membership alone does not establish or defeat licensing authority; MusicBat must verify any applicable carve-outs and obligations.
W3. An unverified, disputed or restricted use remains unavailable until documented clearance. A false warranty may support contractual remedies, suspension and appropriately scoped indemnification subject to applicable law and counsel review, but does not release MusicBat from its own legal obligations or eliminate third-party claims.
W4. Acceptance must capture signer legal identity/capacity, recording-specific schedules, complete version/digest, affirmative consent and server timestamp. A posted template or checkbox without authority is insufficient.

## Addendum X — Optional Independent Business Extensions
X1. Sync opportunities are opt-in only. Interest registration is not a license. Any actual sync deal requires separately executed master and composition permissions, permitted media, territory, duration, consideration, beneficiary allocation and necessary approvals. MusicBat is not appointed as a general agent or manager by this addendum.
X2. MusicBat may preserve accurate and lawful participation, transaction and rights provenance. Public celebration of an artist's milestones requires appropriately scoped consent; marketing use of name, likeness, story or audio requires separate authorization. Participation never assigns future achievements to MusicBat.

## Addendum D — Copyright Complaints and DMCA Operations
D1. MusicBat will maintain a publicly accessible copyright complaint contact and, where applicable, a registered DMCA designated agent, an appropriate notice/counter-notice process, expeditious removal or disabling procedures, repeat-infringer policy and auditable escalation and restoration decisions.
D2. Contractual artist warranties do not replace MusicBat's independent statutory obligations. A claim places only the affected uses and disputed payments on appropriate review hold, subject to law and the existing agreement.

# MusicBat legal governance and execution authority

Classification: Permanent development governance. Owner directive, October 30, 2026.
Repository: ltkrisp-afk/musicbat-live; existing PR #5.

The owner determines the business model, product requirements and authorized scope. Work implements engineering, prepares original legal documents, verifies technical behavior and reports evidence within that scope. Work is not MusicBat's legal counsel. Independent professional review is an external process managed by the owner; Work's assessment is not formal legal approval.

## Separate readiness decisions

- Implementation readiness: whether functionality can be built and tested.
- Legal-review readiness: whether documents and evidence are complete enough for professional evaluation.
- Production activation: whether a live feature or agreement is authorized to operate.

External legal review does not automatically suspend unrelated authorized engineering, documentation, design or tests. Identify the specific uncertainty, affected operation and evidence needed. Continue independent work. Do not invent restrictions, imply counsel rejected unreviewed terms, or label language unlawful without adequate support. Do not enable an operation known to lack required authority.

## Preserve owner decisions and verified work

Preserve artist ownership and independence; properly authorized direct nonexclusive licensing; artist-controlled redistribution; 85/15 purchases; fixed 25% new streaming-pool funding; separate carry-forward liabilities; verified designated recipients; historical agreements and purchase entitlements; sync exclusion. Any proposed deviation needs an explanation and owner authorization.

A documentation issue does not reopen completed design or engineering without a demonstrated dependency. Investigate verified defects, preserve completed work and add relevant regression tests. Do not substitute new proposals for an authorized implementation assignment.

## Professional review package

Maintain traceability: applicable legal requirement → contractual provision → operational control → verification evidence.

Prepare the complete proposed agreements and schedules, relevant authorities and assumptions, implementation map, rights-clearance and accounting specifications, verification outcomes and unresolved questions, and concise matters requiring independent professional judgment. Documents must be original, consistent and adapted to MusicBat. No attorney certification, guaranteed compliance or approval may be claimed without evidence. Lack of attorney approval is not a reason to indefinitely defer otherwise authorized development.

## Boundaries

Existing restrictions on production deployment, contract activation and PR merging remain until separately authorized. This governance document is not a contract amendment, copyright grant, activation record or external legal opinion.

**Build what is authorized. Document what is implemented. Verify what is enforceable. Identify what requires external review.**

**Keep the mystery in the music. Take the mystery out of the business.**

## Continuous, enforceable and proportional governance

Every permission governs its corresponding operation. Every obligation needs an accountable mechanism and evidence. Every restriction affects only the operations its legal or contractual basis requires. An artist's withdrawal of streaming permission must reach the radio engine without silently revoking independently authorized downloads, historical accounting or existing customer entitlements. The artist experiences a coherent music service; the software carries the administrative work underneath it.
